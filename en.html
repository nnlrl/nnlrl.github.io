About

My research focuses on bioinformatics and antimicrobial materials, combining multi-omics data analysis with cellular, microbiological and animal experiments. I received my Ph.D. in Zoology from Inner Mongolia University and completed postdoctoral research in its College of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering.

I have published 11 SCI-indexed papers, including three as first author, and have led one research project and participated in two others.

Bioinformatics & multi-omics

DNA methylation, ChIP-seq, ATAC-seq, RNA-seq and single-cell transcriptomics in embryonic stem cells and developmental biology.

Antimicrobial materials

Antimicrobial polymer development, self-assembly and physicochemical characterization, with biological evaluation using cellular, microbiological and animal experiments.
